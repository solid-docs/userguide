# Manage Access

**Manage access** is the button with a person icon in the header of an item. It is also an entry in the **⋮** menus of a folder and of the items in it. It is there to show who can see or change an item, and to let its owner change that. You only see it when you are logged in.

## What it does in SolidOS 3.1

This is what happened when the button was tried for this guide, in November 2026:

| Where you click it | What happens |
|---|---|
| The header of a single file you have opened | The older [Sharing](/views/sharing) panel opens in place of the file. |
| The **⋮** menu of a folder, or of an item in a folder | Nothing opens. |

So in 3.1 the way to see who has access to something is to open the item itself and use the button in its header.

## Changing who has access

The Sharing panel lists people in groups such as Owners, Editors and Viewers; [Sharing](/views/sharing) describes it.

Whether a change is saved depends on your pod's server. On the test pod used for this guide the server refused the change, and the panel went back to how it was. If that happens to you, the access settings have not changed.

## If you cannot see the button

- You are not logged in. See [Login and Logout](/getting-started/login-logout).
- The window is narrow. The button moves into the **⋮** menu of the header.
