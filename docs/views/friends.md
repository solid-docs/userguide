# Friends

Your profile can list the people you know. SolidOS shows them in the Friends view. The list is part of your public profile, so anyone who can read your profile can see it.

Friends are not the same as [Address Book](/views/addressbook) contacts. Contacts are private and hold details such as phone numbers. Friends are a public list of people.

## Open your friends

1. Log in.
2. Click your picture at the top right to open the account menu.
3. Choose **Friends**.

![The account menu: Profile, Friends, Storage, Dashboard, Preferences, Sign out](/img/ug-views/account-menu.png)

The view shows your name and picture, and two tabs:

- **All Friends**: the people your profile lists.
- **Requests**: people who list you but are not on your list yet (**Incoming requests**), and people on your list who do not list you back (**Awaiting confirmation**). It only looks at profiles SolidOS has already loaded, so it may miss some.

![The Friends view with the All Friends and Requests tabs](/img/ug-views/friends.png)
